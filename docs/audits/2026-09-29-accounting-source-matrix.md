## Source use rules for reviewers

The official rows above are current-source checks for the audit date. A later implementation review must re-open the source pages if the effective period changes, cite the exact paragraph/form instruction used, and keep legal conclusions bounded by executable product evidence. OpenSpec/docs rows describe the product contract and are not substitutes for German law.
